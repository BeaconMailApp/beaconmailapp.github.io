Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Beacon for Gmail ("Beacon") is a free desktop email app for Windows made by Lawrence Rank. This policy explains what Beacon does with your information. The short version: Beacon runs entirely on your computer, connects only to Google, and does not collect, sell or share your data.

What Beacon accesses

When you sign in with your Google account and allow it, Beacon uses Google's APIs to:

  • Gmail: read, send, organize, label, archive and delete your email and drafts, so it can work as your email app.
  • Google Calendar (optional): see your calendars and see, add, change and delete events, so it can show and edit your calendar.
  • Basic account details: your email address, so it can show which account you are using.

Beacon only does these things when you use it, for example to show your inbox, send a message you wrote or carry out an action you chose.

Where your data goes

  • Beacon talks directly from your computer to Google. There is no Beacon server, and the developer never receives your email, calendar, contacts or sign-in.
  • Your Google sign-in is saved on your computer, encrypted by Windows so only your Windows account can read it.
  • To work well, Beacon keeps a few things on your computer only: your settings, a list of people you have emailed (for name suggestions), what the For You feature has learned about which senders matter to you, and a small log file for troubleshooting. None of it leaves your computer.
  • Beacon has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • If you use Unsubscribe in Clean up, Beacon contacts the unsubscribe address the sender provided in their email (a web address or an email address), the same as clicking unsubscribe yourself. It only does this when you ask.
  • Pictures inside emails are blocked by default, so senders cannot tell when you open their mail, unless you choose to show them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Beacon's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide Beacon's features to you. It is not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, not transferred to others, and not read by any person.

Removing your data

  • Remove an account in Beacon's Settings, and Beacon signs out of it and deletes what it stored for that account.
  • Uninstall Beacon from Windows Settings, then delete its folder in %APPDATA% (named Beacon, or Gmail Desk in older versions) to remove everything it kept on your computer.
  • You can also remove Beacon's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Beacon is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, because everything stays on the user's own computer. Anyone under 18 should use Beacon only with a parent's or guardian's permission.
