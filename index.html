The important stuff, first.

Beacon is a free desktop app for Gmail on Windows. It puts the email that matters in front of you, keeps the rest out of the way, and never sends your mail anywhere but Google.

Download for Windows View the code

Beacon is an independent project. It is not made by or affiliated with Google. Gmail is a trademark of Google LLC.

For You

Learns which email matters to you from who you write to and what you open, star and delete. Teach it with one key.

Clean up

See your inbox grouped by sender. Archive, delete or unsubscribe from a whole sender in one click.

Calendar

Day, week and month views of your Google Calendar. Add, edit and answer invites without opening a browser.

Built for the desktop

Tray icon, Windows alerts for important mail only, dark mode, keyboard shortcuts and several Gmail accounts side by side.

Write faster

Name suggestions as you type, paste screenshots straight in, drag files to attach, and undo send.

Free and open

No ads, no account to create, no subscription. The code is public so anyone can see exactly what it does.

Your email stays between you and Google

Beacon runs on your computer and talks directly to Google's Gmail and Calendar services. There is no Beacon server. Nothing about your email is collected, stored elsewhere, sold or shared. Read the privacy policy for the details.
